HOW WE WORK

Editorial policy.

CurioLab is preparing its first documented experiments. Until a result is published with its method and evidence, experiment cards are plans—not findings.

Before publishing a test

  1. Define the task and evaluation criteria in advance.
  2. Record the date, tool version, plan and relevant settings.
  3. Use comparable inputs and preserve representative outputs.
  4. Document limitations, errors, costs and privacy considerations.
  5. Disclose affiliate links or sponsorships clearly if introduced.

Corrections and contact

Before public launch, add a monitored contact email and a clear way to request a correction. Review this policy against the site's actual publishing and monetization setup.
