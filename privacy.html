PRIVACY

Privacy, without guesswork.

This notice describes the current version of CurioLab at the time of publication. The site is a static information website. It does not currently offer an email signup form, user account, or on-site payment form. The interactive tool finder operates in the visitor's browser; its selections are not submitted to a CurioLab application server by that tool.

Hosting and technical requests

The site is hosted using Netlify. Like most web hosting services, the host and related infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL, and timestamps for delivery, security, and operational purposes. Review Netlify's current privacy documentation for details about its processing and retention practices.

The site imports fonts from Google Fonts. Loading these fonts can cause the visitor's browser to make requests to Google's servers. Those requests are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

CurioLab has not intentionally added an analytics product, advertising tag, or advertising network integration in this site package. Hosting or third-party services may still use technical mechanisms needed to deliver or secure their services. This statement should be rechecked after every deployment and before any monetization launch.

If analytics, advertising, affiliate tools, embedded content, or other third-party services are introduced, this notice must be updated to explain the services actually used, the information they process, relevant cookie or consent choices, and how visitors can manage those choices. Children and personal information
