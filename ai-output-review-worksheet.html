What the worksheet records

CriterionWeightReview question
Accuracy and source fidelity35%Are claims supported and qualifications preserved?
Coverage25%Are important points and limitations included?
Clarity15%Is the answer understandable and precise?
Organization10%Is the structure logical and easy to scan?
Revision usefulness15%Does the review identify actionable corrections?

How to use it

  1. Record the task, source version, prompt, tool/version, date, and reviewer. Write “unknown” when a detail cannot be verified.
  2. Score each criterion from 1 to 5 and add a short evidence note. Do not award a score based only on fluent writing.
  3. Calculate each weighted contribution as (rating ÷ 5) × weight, then add the contributions for a total out of 100.
  4. Record missing information, unsupported claims, edits, uncertainty, and relevant conflicts of interest.
  5. If comparing tools, use the same source, task, criteria, and revision rules as far as possible.

Important limitations

A weighted score is a structured judgment, not an objective measure of intelligence or general quality. Different tasks may require different criteria and weights. For high-stakes material, use qualified review and authoritative references; do not rely on an AI output or this worksheet alone.
